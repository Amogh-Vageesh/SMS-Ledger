# SMS Ledger v1.115

Protection transaction linking and maintenance plans.

- Added Ledger category: Protection.
- Added Protection subtypes: Insurance, Warranty, Maintenance.
- Ledger entries can link to a specific insurance policy, warranty, or maintenance plan.
- Added maintenance plans/AMCs with asset linking, cost, dates, services and next-service date.
- Protection screen groups insurance, warranty and maintenance items by category with collapsible sections.
- Protection data is included in complete Family Hub sharing.
- Protection records are persisted in local/cloud settings data.
- Existing transaction/loan/asset linking behavior retained.
